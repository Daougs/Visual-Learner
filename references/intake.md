# Intake questions

Ask with the AskUserQuestion tool (up to 4 questions per call). Skip any question the learner has already answered in their request. The learner can always pick "Other" and type their own answer.

## Call 1 — the course

| Header | Question | Options (first is the default) |
|---|---|---|
| Topic | "What subject do you want to learn, and what's the goal?" (ask only if the request doesn't say) | Offer 2–3 readings of their request, e.g. "Personal finance in India: invest my own money", "…for an exam", "…for work" |
| Complexity | "How complex should the course be?" | Beginner (Recommended if they say they're new): assumes no prior knowledge, defines every term · Intermediate: assumes the basics · Advanced: dense, technical, fewer definitions · Beginner to advanced: starts from zero, ends deep |
| Lesson length | "How long should each video be?" | About 10 minutes (Recommended) · About 5 minutes · About 15 minutes |
| Time frame | "In what time frame do you want to finish the course?" | 2 weeks (Recommended) · 1 week · 1 month · 3 months |

## Call 2 — delivery (only when the course has more than one video)

| Header | Question | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Run time | "What time should the task run?" | 7:00 am · 12:30 pm · 7:00 pm · 10:00 pm |
| Days | "On which days should lessons arrive?" | Every day · Weekdays only · Weekends only |
| Pace | "How many lessons per release?" | 1 a day (Recommended) · 2 a day · 3 a week |

Also confirm the time zone. Use what the learner states; if you can't tell, ask in the same call ("Which time zone are you in?" with their likely zone first).

Videos are delivered by the scheduled task when it runs, so the run time is when the lesson starts building; it lands roughly 30–40 minutes later (research check, audio, recording). Say so.

## Call 3 — context (only when it matters)

- **Region**: for money, law, tax, medicine, exams and anything that differs by country.
- **Background**: what they already know (e.g. "Grade 12 economics"), so chapters can link to it.
- **Voice**: the default narrator is Kokoro "af_heart" (warm US English). Offer another Kokoro voice if they want, and note any premium voice they'd like for later.

## Unattended runs

If no one can answer, choose: Beginner, about 10 minutes, 2 weeks, 1 lesson a day, 7:00 am in the learner's likely time zone. State these choices at the top of the work.

## Turning answers into a plan

- Lessons = release days in the time frame × lessons per release. Round to a structure the subject supports; if the subject needs more room than the time frame allows, say so and offer either more lessons per release or a longer time frame.
- Narration budget ≈ 150 spoken words per minute of video, minus about 2 seconds of lead-in and tail per scene.
- Complexity sets the chapter style: Beginner defines everything and uses intuition lines and small worked examples; Advanced keeps definitions to a glossary and spends the time on derivations, edge cases and harder problems.
